**About Bloom**  
At Bloom Early Learning Center, we believe childhood should be where children grow through play, purpose, and growing curious minds, nurturing kind hearts, and building a love of learning. Our goal is to create more than a place for children to spend their day—we want to build a community where every family is known, loved, and encouraged to learn, play, grow, and flourish. Bloom will have four thoughtfully designed classrooms serving infants through pre-K. We are passionate about providing quality care and a foundation for learning. We believe that children thrive when they feel safe, loved, and connected. When a child is in that environment, they are set up to thrive naturally\! Our classrooms will be filled with opportunities to play, create, explore, get messy, and solve problems. We will use Creative Curriculum, which focuses on building relationships, play, and a supportive environment for young children and suggests that children learn best by actively exploring and playing in a well-designed environment. We will also use Handwriting Without Tears, an occupational therapy-based program designed to build fine motor skills, hand strength, and correct letter formation using multisensory methods. What makes Bloom truly special goes beyond our curriculum. It will be the relationships, the teachers who genuinely know and love the children in their classrooms, and the little moments we celebrate every day. We want children to be excited to walk through our doors, and families to feel confident and connected. Together we’ll Bloom.
